Why Donate Blood?

A single donation can assist multiple patients when separated into red blood cells, plasma, and platelets. Maintaining a healthy flow of donors ensures emergencies are handled without access delay.

✓ Keeps hearts healthy and lowers cancer risk
✓ Free health screening and blood profile lookup
✓ Ultimate joy of giving back and saving 3 lives

Donor Eligibility

Before signing up, make sure you satisfy standard safety requirements so donations are beneficial and risk-free:

⚖ Weight above 50 kg (110 lbs)
🎂 Age between 18 and 60 years old
📅 Last donated date must be at least 4 months ago
